# Portfolio Design Specification

## Governing aesthetic

**Modern scientific practice housed within a restrained ceremonial order.**

The historical influence should be felt through proportion, hierarchy, inscription, rubric, symmetry, spacing and selective ornament. The subject remains contemporary data analytics, data science, machine learning and data engineering.

The site should remain **technical first, cultivated second, decorative third**.

Writing follows the same rule: **majesty through control**. Use short to medium sentences, one principal idea at a time, concrete nouns, active verbs and exact technical language. Avoid consultancy boilerplate, decorative abstraction and long subordinate chains unless the thought requires them.

## Semantic colour system

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Deep Cathedral Green | `#123D32` | Identity, primary actions, major CTA surfaces |
| Oxford Blue | `#142B4A` | Technical intelligence, diagrams, code, primary chart series |
| Prayerbook Rubric Red | `#8B2C2F` | Editorial and scholarly emphasis |
| Antique Gold | `#B3924A` | Ceremony, ornament, benchmarks and selected references |
| Charcoal | `#252622` | Principal text and structural neutral |
| Ivory | `#F7F3E9` | Primary page ground |
| Parchment | `#EDE4D2` | Secondary surfaces and quiet panels |

Rubric Red is reserved for Notes, article categories, scholarly markers, active reading states and occasional intellectual emphasis. It must not be used for primary CTAs, project CTAs, main navigation, capability cards or default chart series.

## Chart semantics

- Oxford Blue: primary data series.
- Blue/teal derived from Oxford Blue: secondary or comparison series.
- Antique Gold: benchmark, target, threshold, selected observation or reference line.
- Charcoal or reduced-opacity neutral: supporting context.
- Rubric Red: exceptional editorial meaning only, never a routine data series.

## Ornament constitution

**Ornament is punctuation, never wallpaper.**

Ornament may appear on:
- the Home outer frame, very lightly;
- the Case Study title / executive-summary area;
- a major collaboration CTA;
- the monogram;
- the About lancet arch.

Ornament must disappear from:
- Work page outer corners;
- Notes page outer corners;
- project cards;
- capability cards;
- charts;
- article rows;
- ordinary data visualisations.

Practical rule: **no more than one ornamental event per major viewport, excluding the monogram.**

Architectural forms are events, not wallpaper. The lancet belongs to About and appears nowhere else.

## Typography

- Display / headings: Playfair Display, with EB Garamond and Georgia fallbacks.
- Body / UI: Inter, with system sans-serif fallbacks.
- Display typography may carry ceremony. Body typography must remain compact and highly readable.

## Home

### Hero

Name is the dominant object. A softly integrated global data-intelligence field sits behind the right side of the hero. The world map, regional colouring, network lines and analytical marks must blend into the ivory field rather than read as a separate dashboard. The portrait appears as a restrained circular identity element over the field.

Professional scope:

**Data Analytics · Data Science · Machine Learning · Data Engineering**

Hook:

**Analytics that inform. Models that forecast. Systems built to scale.**

Homepage identity rules:
- distribute the four professional disciplines evenly in a full-width rail beneath the header;
- use the same four Antique Gold line icons in the discipline rail and Practice section;
- integrate portrait and global intelligence field as one composition;
- keep the map richly saturated and high-contrast, with continent labels visibly tethered to their geography;
- package Work with me, View work and Résumé as equal hero actions;
- Résumé links directly to the uploaded PDF and never has its own page;
- use proper Email, LinkedIn, GitHub and Location marks wherever those contact methods appear;
- public location: Nairobi.

### Selected Work

- Customer Churn Intelligence — Predictive analytics · ML
- Retail Data Pipeline — ETL · Orchestration
- Demand Forecasting — Time Series · ML

Project CTAs use Charcoal with Cathedral Green hover. Never Rubric Red.

### Practice

- Analytics & BI: clear analysis and decision-ready dashboards.
- Data Engineering: reliable, scalable data pipelines and infrastructure.
- Data Science: statistical analysis, experimentation and structured inference.
- Machine Learning: prediction, evaluation and scalable workflows.

Each area uses a matched Antique Gold line icon. The heading is **Practice**, not Capabilities.

The four Practice areas sit inside one complete framed table: a fine Antique Gold perimeter, continuous internal rules, restrained field numbering and a quiet lower accent in each cell. The frame must remain visually closed at every breakpoint rather than trailing off after the final row.


The homepage then resolves directly into Selected Work, Practice and the site footer.

## Work

The Work page is the least ornamented major page. No outer corner flourishes, lancets or decorative frames.

Heading: **Selected Work**

Optional supporting line: **Selected analyses, systems and models.**

Cards should lead with problem, approach and result. Technology is secondary evidence.

## Case Studies

Use one restrained ornamental event in the title / executive-summary region only.

Recommended structure:
1. Executive Summary
2. Problem / Business Need
3. Data
4. Approach
5. Architecture
6. Model / Method
7. Evaluation
8. Model Results
9. Business Implications
10. Observed Impact, only when genuine deployment evidence exists
11. Limitations
12. Improvements / Next Steps
13. Repository

Never fabricate commercial impact or infer it directly from model metrics.

## Notes

Notes is the editorial heart of the site.

- Article list on one side.
- One corresponding visual preview at a time.
- Active article, category and reading links may use Rubric Red.
- Technical visuals remain principally Oxford Blue.

## About

The About page is a literal three-column editorial composition inside an ivory page sheet on a dark Oxford Blue / Cathedral Green surround. The centre lancet is the visual sovereign of the page; the left and right rails balance it.

### Desktop architecture

- Left editorial rail: rubric marker, **ABOUT**, stacked **PEOPLE / DATA / SYSTEMS / IMPACT**, a short introductory line, section index, and the inscription.
- Centre lancet: Antique Gold outer crown and rails, fine Oxford Blue inner crown and rails, portrait niche, SM medallion, name, discipline line, centred About copy, 2 × 2 Focus / Approach / Interests / Collaboration grid, Cathedral Green collaboration CTA, contact strip, and bottom ornament.
- Right information rail: Currently Exploring timeline, Tech Stack icon grid, and Beyond Work. The Tech Stack maintains a three-up rhythm; when the item count leaves two items on the final row, those two balance the full width deliberately.

The gold and Oxford Blue lancet rails continue uninterrupted from the crown to the base. The centre panel uses fine rules and continuous grids, not floating cards.

### Mobile architecture

At phone widths, the side rails disappear. The centre lancet becomes the page: crown, portrait, identity, About copy, four stacked sections, collaboration CTA and contact strip. The crown and vertical rails remain visible.

### About copy

**I work with data as evidence and engineering as craft.**

My practice spans data analytics, data science, machine learning and data engineering. One concern governs the work: to turn complex information into systems that are intelligible, dependable and scalable.

I am interested in the whole chain of reasoning, from schema and query to pipeline, model and deployed AI system. I am partial to work that can be explained, tested, reproduced and improved.

### Focus

**End-to-end analytical and data systems.**

From SQL and analytical reporting to reliable data pipelines, statistical modelling, forecasting and machine learning.

### Approach

**Rigour before spectacle.**

Sound data, explicit assumptions, careful evaluation and engineering that remains legible under scrutiny.

### Interests

**Systems that reason over evidence.**

Data modelling, machine learning, retrieval systems, language models, analytical systems and dependable infrastructure.

### Collaboration

**Serious work, clearly made.**

Open to employment, freelance and collaborative work where technical depth, disciplined execution and clear communication matter.

### Inscription

**Evidence first. Structure before scale. Intelligence as consequence.**

### Currently Exploring

- Retrieval and LLM systems grounded in reliable data
- Data pipelines built for observability and reproducibility
- Machine learning systems beyond notebook-scale experimentation
- Reliable deployment patterns for machine-learning workflows

### Tech Stack

The visible stack includes Python, SQL, Microsoft Excel, pandas, Polars, scikit-learn, XGBoost, PyTorch, Airflow, Docker, AWS, MySQL, Power BI and Git. Each technology has a local icon asset; pandas and Polars remain distinct entries.

### Beyond Work

**Outside technical work, I read widely, study languages and history, and return often to music, theology, philosophy and literature. I value long walks through unfamiliar places.**

### Availability

**Employment · Freelance · Collaboration**

Availability is a compact Contact-page status band using the same framed ivory surface, Antique Gold corner treatment and Oxford Blue display typography as the rest of the site. The heading appears once, followed directly by Employment, Freelance and Collaboration as three equal status fields; no explanatory sentence repeats those modes.

## Responsive behaviour

Design deliberately for wide desktop, laptop, tablet landscape, tablet portrait, Android mobile and iPhone mobile.

- Hero: two columns on desktop, single reading flow on mobile.
- Selected Work: three columns to one.
- Practice: four columns, then two, then one.
- Case Study: side navigation becomes horizontal or collapsible.
- Notes: article list and visual preview stack on mobile.
- About: three-column editorial composition on desktop; on mobile, the side rails disappear and the centre lancet becomes the complete reading flow while its crown and continuous rails remain intact.
- Maintain 44 px minimum touch targets.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- No horizontal page overflow.

## Content integrity

No fabricated metrics, client names, business impact or deployment claims.

Independent projects may show **Model Results** and **Business Implications**. **Observed Impact** appears only when genuine real-world evidence exists.


### Lancet frame construction
The central About-page lancet is rendered as two continuous SVG paths: an Antique Gold outer path and an Oxford Blue inset path. Each path contains the crown, shoulders, vertical rails, lower corners and base in one closed geometric system. Do not reconstruct the frame from separate CSS borders or line fragments.


## About page interaction and section parity (V8)

The About index must name only sections that actually exist on the page, in the same wording used by the visible content: **Overview, Focus, Approach, Interests, Collaboration, Currently Exploring, Tech Stack, Beyond Work**. Do not use proxy labels such as Experience, Education, or Tools & Skills unless those sections are later added explicitly.

On desktop and tablet, the index functions as in-page navigation and the active item updates as the reader scrolls. Rubric Red marks the current location. Anchor scrolling accounts for the sticky site header. On mobile, the editorial side rail remains hidden to preserve the approved phone composition. Motion is restrained and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## v13 homepage identity and CMS rule

The Home hero no longer repeats the name as oversized display type. The site masthead carries the name; the hero carries the person and the work.

Home hierarchy:
1. professional disciplines
2. portrait
3. `Analytics that inform. Models that forecast. Systems built to scale.`
4. Work With Me / View Work
5. atmospheric global data-intelligence field

The global visual is a background intelligence field, not a dashboard or explanatory diagram. It must remain subordinate to the identity and copy.

### CMS boundary

Content and replaceable media are CMS-managed. Visual constitution remains code-managed.

CMS-managed image slots include the brand mark, homepage data field, profile portrait, project cover images, Note images, technology icons and optional capability icons. A replacement image must propagate automatically on the next build without editing HTML or CSS.

## v14 interaction contract

- `Work with me` always opens the dedicated Contact page.
- `Résumé` is a first-class navigation/footer item and remains a deliberate placeholder until a PDF is supplied.
- Each showcased project presents two adjacent actions: **Read more** and **GitHub**.
- **Read more** always resolves to a generated project article/detail page.
- **GitHub** links to the project's repository when supplied; missing repository URLs display as pending, never as broken anchors.
