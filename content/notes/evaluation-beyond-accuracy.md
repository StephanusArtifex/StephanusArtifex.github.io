## Working thesis

Accuracy is useful only when the error distribution and decision threshold fit the problem. Evaluation should reflect the cost of false positives, false negatives and uncertainty.

## Planned scope

- Confusion matrices.
- Precision, recall and F1.
- Thresholds and calibration.
- ROC and precision-recall curves.
