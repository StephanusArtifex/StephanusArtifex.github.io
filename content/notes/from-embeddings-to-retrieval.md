## Working thesis

Embeddings convert semantic relationships into geometric structure. Retrieval systems make that structure operational by ranking useful context against a query.

## What this note will examine

- Representation and similarity.
- Cosine similarity and nearest-neighbour search.
- Chunking and retrieval quality.
- Evaluation beyond visually pleasing demos.
