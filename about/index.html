I work with data as evidence and engineering as craft.

My practice spans data analytics, data science, machine learning and data engineering. One concern governs the work: to turn complex information into systems that are intelligible, dependable and scalable.

I am interested in the full data lifecycle, from schema and query to pipelines, models and operational use. I am partial to work that can be explained, tested, reproduced and improved.

Focus

End-to-end analytical and data systems. From SQL and analytical reporting to reliable data pipelines, statistical modelling, forecasting and machine learning.

Approach

Rigour before spectacle. Sound data, explicit assumptions, careful evaluation and engineering that remains legible under scrutiny.

Interests

Systems built around evidence. Data modelling, statistical analysis, forecasting, machine learning, data pipelines and the engineering needed to keep them dependable.

Collaboration

Serious work, clearly made. Open to employment, freelance and collaborative work where technical depth, disciplined execution and clear communication matter.
